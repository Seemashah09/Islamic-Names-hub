About Us

Welcome to IslamicNamesHub.com

IslamicNamesHub.com is your trusted resource for finding beautiful and meaningful Islamic names for your children. We understand that choosing a name is one of the most important decisions parents make, as it becomes a lifelong identity for your child.

Our mission is to provide a comprehensive, accurate, and easy-to-use platform where parents can explore Islamic names from various origins — Arabic, Quranic, Urdu, and more — complete with their meanings in English, Urdu, and Arabic.

What We Offer

  • Thousands of Islamic names with verified meanings
  • Names from the Holy Quran with references
  • Meanings in English, Urdu, and Arabic
  • Easy search and filtering tools
  • Informative articles and guides
